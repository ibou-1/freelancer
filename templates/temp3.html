Our story

Maison Aurèle began in a small room with one treatment table and a belief that skincare should feel unhurried.

We draw on Moroccan beauty traditions, from argan oil to rose water and the hammam, and pair them with modern skin science. Every product is chosen for what it does, and every treatment is shaped around the person in the chair.

We see a small number of guests each day, so each one has our full attention.
